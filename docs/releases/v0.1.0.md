The first public preview of AI Video Editor: record, cut with AI, polish and export, in one desktop app.

## Highlights

- **Recording**: whole screen or a dragged area, with a microphone, saved losslessly. Interrupted takes can be recovered.
- **Magic Edit**: a first cut from a local transcript that removes long pauses, filler words and repeated takes.
- **Editing**: trim, split, range cuts, restore, zooms on a clicked spot, and 2×–8× speed-ups that keep the voice's pitch. Every change, manual or AI, can be undone.
- **AI assistant**: connect Claude (through Claude Code, the default), Codex with a ChatGPT account, or an OpenAI, Gemini or DeepSeek API key.
- **Captions and audio**: captions from the transcript (preview, .srt or burned in), loudness normalisation and noise reduction.
- **Export**: a verified lossless master or a smaller MP4, and vertical, square or landscape short clips.

## Download

`ai-video-editor-linux-x64.tar.gz` contains the app for 64-bit Linux with X11. Extract it and run `ai-video-editor`. FFmpeg and ffprobe must be installed and on your `PATH`. Check the download against `SHA256SUMS`.

To build from source, see the [getting started guide](https://github.com/klimPaskov/ai-video-editor/blob/main/docs/guide/getting-started.md).

## Known limits

- Linux with X11 only. Windows packaging is in progress; macOS is not supported.
- Recording a single window, system audio and a camera are not available yet.
- The build is not code-signed.
