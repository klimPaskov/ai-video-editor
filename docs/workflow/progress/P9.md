# P9 progress

## 2026-10-07 Verified draft export (P9-03 partial, P9-04)

The committed draft now exports through the Export step. See `docs/18_EXPORT_AND_DELIVERY.md` for the canonical render definition, the refused formats and the verification sequence.

Evidence: the media tests compare a cut, reordered two-source H.264/AAC master against an independent full decode without seeking, plus BGRA/16-bit PCM, MP4, refusal and cancellation cases. The packaged native test passed in the cloud VM: import, a real range cut, lossless master and MP4 export through the Export step, independent frame and audio equality of the master, cancellation without a file, unchanged source; screenshots were reviewed and led to a compact quality row.

Findings during the slice: decoding AAC after an input seek did not reproduce the samples of a full decode even with timestamps kept, so audio is decoded once per source from its start; Matroska's random segment ID made identical renders differ, so the master is muxed bit-exact.
