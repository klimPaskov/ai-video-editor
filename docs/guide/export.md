# Export and short clips

## Export

In **Export**, choose a quality:

- **Lossless master** (default): Matroska with FFV1 video and PCM audio. Every frame and sample of the edit is kept exactly, and the app checks the file after writing it. Files are large.
- **Smaller file**: an MP4 (H.264 and AAC) for sharing.

Choose **Export…** and pick where to save. Zooms, speed-ups, burned-in captions and audio cleanup are included. With captions on, an .srt file is saved next to the video. You can cancel an export; nothing is saved in that case.

## Short clips

Under **Short clips**, choose **Find clips**. The app looks through the transcript of the edit for self-contained 15–60 second moments that start at the beginning of a sentence and end on a complete one. Each suggestion shows its title, length, an excerpt and why it was chosen.

For each clip you can **Preview** it, **Discard** it, or **Export clip** as:

- **Vertical 9:16**, **Square 1:1** or **Landscape 16:9**
- **Fit with blurred background** or **Fill the frame**, with an adjustable crop position
- captions burned in, placed clear of the controls social apps draw at the bottom
- the clip's own **title** shown over its first seconds, if you turn **Show title** on
- normal or **2× speed** (the voice keeps its pitch)

Instead of the app's own rules, choose **Pick with Claude** to let Claude choose the clips. It only reads your edit and proposes up to six; nothing in your edit changes, and its picks are marked as Claude's.

**Export all** saves every clip into one folder you choose, one after another. Clips you have exported are listed under the suggestions by file name, with **Show in folder** and **Remove from list** (which forgets the entry and keeps the file).
