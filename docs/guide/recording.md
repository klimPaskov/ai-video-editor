# Recording

Choose **New recording** on Home.

1. **Screen**: pick a display.
2. **Area**: **Whole screen**, or **Part of the screen**. For part of the screen, drag a rectangle over the display and press Enter or **Record this area**. Escape cancels. The chosen size is shown in the dialog.
3. **Microphone**: pick a microphone, or **No microphone**.
4. Choose **Start recording**. After a 3-2-1 countdown, recording starts.

While recording, you can **Pause** and **Resume**. **Stop** saves the take, names it after the date and time, and opens it as a new project.

## Quality

Recordings are saved losslessly: FFV1 video at 30 frames per second and 16-bit PCM audio at 48 kHz. The microphone is aligned to the first video frame, so pausing and resuming keeps sound and picture in sync. If the computer cannot keep up, the dialog shows how many frames were missed.

## If the app quits during a recording

Recording stops together with the app. On the next launch, Home shows **A recording from … was interrupted**. Choose **Recover** to open everything captured up to that point, or **Discard** to delete it.

## Current limits

Recording a single window, system audio and a camera are not available yet. On Linux, recording needs an X11 session.
